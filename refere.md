- To conclude with vision-based fall detection, the study conducted by Ahmed et al. (2025) also focused on fall detection using MediaPipe and handcrafted features. However, the system was intended to detect falls in people of different ages and genders within indoor environments. The system detects the initial nose point of the individual and also uses a threshold to determine a fall on the features: head angle, distance between nose and left ankle, percentage change of nose to left ankle with respect to initial nose point, are then calculated after fall detection. Though the proposed system when tested with the UR Fall Dataset achieved 100% accuracy, it was noted that MediaPipe in itself had some limitations as it was not able to detect falls from top view. Also, the model was not able to detect multiple people falling. In addition, lighting played a major role, as the thresholds were different in different lighting conditions and some complex situations.
